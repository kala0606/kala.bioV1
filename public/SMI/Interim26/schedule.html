02

The day

Every day has the same shape: talk in the morning, make through the middle, and end at the wall.

9:30–10:15
Gathering

A talk, demo or reading.

10:15–12:30
Studio

Individual work with Codex while the facilitator circulates. Week 2 technique clinics run here.

12:30–1:15
Lunch

 

1:15–2:45
Making

Experimentation, tests and fabrication. Machine slots are booked from Week 3.

2:45–3:30
Pin-up

Outputs on the wall. Peer review in fours. Write the log, file the form, commit.

9:3012:303:30

The wall builds up across the month. By Day 15 it should show every identity's direction, so the cohort reads as one archive, not twenty separate projects.

Machines

Twenty students share one A2 plotter, one resin printer and the in-house archival printer. At Critique 2 on Day 10, each student chooses one pathway or several. No one has to produce all three. From Day 11 every machine is booked through a shared queue.

A2 pen plotter

Available
One machine. Arrives on campus in Week 2. Demo on Day 11.
Capacity
One plot at a time.
Queue
Two slots a day (morning and afternoon) on Days 11–18, so 16 slots. That covers one test and one final for each of 6–8 plotter students.

Resin printer

Available
From Day 11.
Capacity
One print a day, two at most.
Queue
Days 11–14: one test print per resin student. Days 15–18: finals. Four students fit at one print a day; six need two prints a day. Day 18 is the last day a final can print, so washing and curing finish before installation.

Archival printer

Available
In-house, throughout.
Capacity
Fast; not the bottleneck.
Queue
Test strips and proofs on Days 12–13, finals on Days 16–17. Up to 20 hero prints. It is also the fallback for anyone whose plot or resin piece fails.
04

Week 1

depth 1
I

Identity — take a position

Days 1–5 · Mon 23 – Fri 27 Nov

By Friday every student speaks from one authored identity: a named practitioner tied to a real condition of the world they will graduate into. Displacement is used as a lens on that world, not a way out of it.

1Mon 23 Nov

Enter the theme and see what the studio can make. Today

Opening talk: Out of One's Place. A walk through the plotter, resin printer and archival printer, with past work. Clone the starter repo.

Coordinates exercise: list the places, languages, roles and routes you already hold.

Due by 3:30Coordinates sheet. Repo set up with README.+ Daily log
2Tue 24 Nov

Read displacement as a critical lens. Today

Reading: Pessoa's heteronyms and Duchamp's Rrose Sélavy, authored selves with full bodies of work.

Conditions exercise: choose a real condition (water, labour, migration, language, automation, the city, care) and ask who is shaped by it.

Due by 3:30Three candidate identities, a paragraph each, each tied to a condition.+ Daily log
3Wed 25 Nov

Commit to one identity and give it facts. Today

Identity worksheet: the condition, the position, the facts, what it attends to and refuses, its beliefs and rules. Pair interviews: a partner questions the identity for 20 minutes.

Codex as interlocutor: five computational metaphors, no code yet.

Due by 3:30Identity worksheet as identity.md. Codex metaphor log in the repo.+ Daily log
4Thu 26 Nov

Write in the identity's voice. Today

Manifesto workshop: 150 words, read aloud, edited by cutting.

Field research: real archives, materials, sites and data from the identity's world, not a style moodboard.

Due by 3:30Manifesto draft. references.md with at least 15 references.+ Daily log
5Fri 27 Nov

Defend the identity. Critique 1 Today

Critique runs all day. Students not presenting revise and draft the identity card and a one-page proposal with an initial generative concept.

Due by 3:30Identity card draft, manifesto v2 and one-page proposal.+ Daily log

Week 2

depth 2
II

Computation — write the rules

Days 6–10 · Mon 30 Nov – Fri 4 Dec

By Friday every identity has a working system. The system needs parameters, constraints and controlled randomness, and must produce at least 20 outputs. It should not be possible to mistake its rules for someone else's.

6Mon 30 Nov

Turn a metaphor into rules. Today

Talk: what a generative system is (inputs, rules, parameters, randomness, seed). Codex as researcher: the algorithms behind your chosen metaphor.

Clinics: noise and flow fields; recursion and L-systems.

Due by 3:30System diagram on paper: inputs, rules, parameters, outputs. Technique chosen.+ Daily log
7Tue 1 Dec

Get a first working system. Today

Clinics: agents and simulation; cellular automata; data as material, using real datasets from the identity's condition.

Codex as collaborator: propose the architecture before writing code. Seeds and reproducibility.

Due by 3:30The system runs and exports. Seed logged.+ Daily log
8Wed 2 Dec

Map the parameter space. Today

Twenty Variations of Myself: twenty seeds or settings. How far can the system change and still be this identity?

Due by 3:3020 outputs in outputs/digital. Parameter table in the README.+ Daily log
9Thu 3 Dec

Take critique, including the machine's. Today

Codex as critic: analyse the outputs without erasing the voice. Authorship log: what Codex suggested, what you rejected and why.

Peer-group review in fours.

Due by 3:30Authorship log entry. Three promising directions.+ Daily log
10Fri 4 Dec

Show that the system speaks for the identity. Critique 2 Today

Critique runs all day. Students not presenting choose a fabrication pathway and book machine time.

Due by 3:30Pathway or pathways chosen (plotter, resin, archival, or a combination), with a one-page production plan.+ Daily log

Week 3

depth 3
III

Materialisation — give it a body

Days 11–15 · Mon 7 – Fri 11 Dec

By Friday every student has a locked production file and a physical prototype. Each student works in one medium or several: only plots, only prints, a resin object, or all three. Capacity is 6–8 A2 plots and 4–6 resin objects. The archival printer can take everyone, so it is the fallback.

11Mon 7 Dec

Learn the constraints of each medium. Today

Demos in rotation. Plotter: A2 is 420 × 594 mm; covers SVG, path ordering, pen lifts, nibs and paper. Resin: watertight meshes, wall thickness, supports, safe handling. Archival: 300 PPI at final size, colour profiles, TIFF or PDF.

Due by 3:30Production spec sheet for your medium.+ Daily log
12Tue 8 Dec

Make a production-ready file. Today

Codex as fabricator: export pipeline (SVG optimisation, STL repair, high-resolution TIFF). Resin test prints begin, one a day.

Due by 3:30First file passes its checks: path-optimised, watertight or 300 PPI.+ Daily log
13Wed 9 Dec

Test material against the idea. Today

Test plots on two papers and two nibs. Resin test washed and cured. Archival colour proof and test strip.

Due by 3:30Test log with photos and notes.+ Daily log
14Thu 10 Dec

Build a prototype. Today

The hero piece at reduced scale or as a section. Draft process.md, explaining the computational logic in your own words.

Due by 3:30Prototype and a process.md draft.+ Daily log
15Fri 11 Dec

Show that the material carries the system. Critique 3 Today

Critique runs all day. Students not presenting lock the production file and write an exhibition proposal (what is shown in the zone and how).

Due by 3:30Final production file locked. Exhibition proposal.+ Daily log

16Mon 14 Dec

Final archival prints. A2 and resin finals continue in their queues. Artist statement workshop. Each student gets coordinates on the Collective Atlas.

Due by 3:30Production under way. Statement draft.+ Daily log
17Tue 15 Dec

Finish the artefact and frame the identity. Today

Resin post-processing, print checks, mounting (framing to be confirmed). Identity cards, labels and QR codes linking to each repo.

Due by 3:30Identity card, label and QR code, each tested by scanning.+ Daily log
18Wed 16 Dec

Document the work. Today

Due by 3:30Repo complete. Work photographed.+ Daily log

Hang the hero works and set out the zones (identity card, manifesto, QR code, artefact). Assemble the Collective Atlas. Lighting and technical tests.

Due by 3:30Zone installed and checked against the installation checklist.+ Daily log

Due by 3:30Final submission checklist complete.+ Daily log
08

Critiques

Each critique takes the full day with one facilitator: 9:30–12:30 and 1:15–2:45, about 13 minutes per student. The student's peer group of four sits in. Students who aren't presenting keep working in the studio.

3 present8 questions2 notes

13 minutes per student, 20 students, one room.

Critique 1 · Day 5 · Fri 27 Nov

Who is speaking? What do they care about? Would this voice matter outside the studio?

Bring identity worksheet, manifesto, references, the condition it answers to, the week’s log on one A4 sheet.

Critique 2 · Day 10 · Fri 4 Dec

Does the system speak in the identity's voice, or could anyone's rules have made this?

Bring running system, 20 outputs, parameter table, authorship log, the week’s log on one A4 sheet.

Critique 3 · Day 15 · Fri 11 Dec

Does the physical work embody the system, and does the material carry the identity's position?

Bring prototype, test log, production file, exhibition proposal, the week’s log on one A4 sheet.
