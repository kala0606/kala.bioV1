How they run

Each critique takes the full day: 9:30–12:30 and 1:15–2:45, about 13 minutes per student, with your peer group of four in the room. Everyone else keeps working in the studio.

3 minutes

You present

The position, the work, and the one thing you most need feedback on.

8 minutes

Questions

The facilitator and your peers ask. You answer; you don't defend.

2 minutes

Notes

Everyone writes. You leave with the notes, and one next step.

Critique 1 assesses A1 (20%) and Critique 2 assesses A2 (30%), both against the rubrics on the assessment page. Critique 3 isn't marked: it's feedback on the artefact before A3 (50%).

—

Three minutes

  1. Who is speaking.

    The identity, its condition and its position, in two sentences. Don't read the manifesto unless it's Critique 1.

  2. What's on the wall.

    Point to the work. Say what it is and how it was made.

  3. Your question.

    The one thing you most need the room to think about. "Is it good?" doesn't count.

Pin up before your slot: the work, plus that week's log on one A4 sheet so reviewers can read it while you set up.

—

Peer notes

In the last two minutes, each person in the room writes three lines and hands them over.

Notes card
For: [identity name] · Critique [1/2/3] · From: [your name]

What holds: [the strongest thing, specifically]
What's thin: [where the position or the work goes vague]
A question: [one you couldn't ask in the time]

Write about the work and the position, not the person. Be specific enough that the note is useful tomorrow morning.

Critique 3

Does the physical work embody the system, and does the material carry the identity's position?

Feedback only. The same questions are assessed in A3.

Bring: prototype, test log, production file, exhibition proposal, the week's log on one A4 sheet.

After the critique

  1. Photograph the notes cards.

    Into log/, the same day.

  2. Write that day's log entry about the critique.

    Decided: the one change you'll make first, and why the identity would make it.

  3. Start Monday with it.

    The next week's first day begins from the critique, not from where you were before it.
