What a system is

A generative system is a set of rules that makes work you could not have drawn one piece at a time, all of it recognisably in one language.

1

Inputs

What comes in: data, a drawing, a text, time, or nothing at all.

2

Rules

The steps, in order. This is where the identity's position lives.

3

Parameters

The dials: values you can change without changing the rules.

4

Chance

Controlled randomness from a seed, so any result can be made again.

5

Outputs

Many works, each saved with the seed and settings that made it.

  • The rules come from the manifesto. If a rule has no reason in the identity's position, question it.
  • You can explain every line. Codex can write the code with you, but at Critique 2 you explain each rule in your own words, without the screen.
  • Image generation is not a system. Prompting an AI image model, on its own, doesn't count. Your rules have to do the work.

A worked example

Anjana Rao's system, from the identity and manifesto examples. Abridged.

The deficit survey

Example
Metaphor
The survey grid as a sieve: each cell keeps the water that should be there.
Inputs
Annual rainfall for Bengaluru, 1990–2024 (CSV). One tank's boundary, traced from an old survey sheet as a polygon.
Rules
  1. Lay a survey grid over the tank boundary.
  2. For each year, take the deficit: the long-run mean minus that year's rainfall.
  3. Give each year one row of horizontal lines across the tank bed.
  4. Draw each row heavier as the deficit grows; years with surplus are drawn faintest.
  5. Stop every line at the boundary. Nothing is drawn outside what was measured.
  6. Write the year and the data source in the margin beside each row.
Parameters
NameControlsRangeReason
cellgrid size5–20 mthe scale a surveyor would walk
deficit_scalepen passes per 100 mm of deficit0.5–3absence drawn heavier than presence
jitterline wobble0–0.4 mma hand, not a machine
yearsfirst and last year1990–2024the span of the records
Chance
Only the jitter is random, from the seed. The data is never randomised: "no water I have not measured."
Technique
Data as material, on a geometric grid. Avoids the default by refusing colour and gradients.
Material
A2 pen plot. The pen's repeated passes make the deficit physical: heavier years are literally more ink.
