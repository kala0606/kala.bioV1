A server is a kitchen that's open

Not a special box. Any computer waiting for orders.

Your laptop becomes one the moment Codex runs python3 -m http.server 8000 in your folder. Close it and the kitchen closes.

The point for this studio

Generative art is cooked at your table.

GitHub Pages only hands over packed files. The drawing happens in each visitor's browser, on their machine, from your rules and their seed.

Do I need a server?

Level 1 · no server

A recipe card in your pocket

Double-click the HTML. Everything is in one file. file://

Level 2 · static server

A takeaway counter

Hands out packed boxes, never cooks. Needed for modules, loaded files, a web address. Your press lives here.

Level 3 · backend

A real kitchen

Secrets, shared galleries, logins, heavy jobs. Most generative work never needs it.
