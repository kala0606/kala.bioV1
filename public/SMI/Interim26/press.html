Your press

Every project is a press: a web page where you, and later any visitor, set the dials, choose a seed, see the result and download it for the plotter or the printer. It comes with the starter and goes online as your project's own site.

02

The parts

Two files are yours. Everything else is the press itself, and stays as it is unless you ask Codex to restyle it.

your-project/ ├── index.html the press page ├── src/system.js YOUR RULES: the draw() function ├── config/params.json YOUR DIALS: name, range, default, reason ├── press/ the press: leave it alone │ ├── pen.js drawing in millimetres, seeded chance, noise │ ├── press.js the controls, preview, sheet, downloads │ └── render.mjs renders a batch of seeds into outputs/ ├── manifesto.md shown on the About tab └── process.md shown on the About tab

This is the same architecture for everyone, so you spend Week 2 on rules, not on setting up software. The system worksheet still decides what the rules are.

03

What to say

Like git, you work the press by asking Codex.

SayCodex
"preview"Starts the press on your laptop and gives you a link to open.
"make a dial for …"Adds a parameter to config/params.json with a sensible range, uses it in your rules, and asks you for the identity's reason.
"change rule 3 so …"Edits src/system.js, one rule at a time, and explains the change.
"render twenty"Writes twenty outputs, named by seed, into outputs/digital/.
"publish"Pushes, makes sure the site is on, and gives you its link. It updates about a minute later.
"restyle the press for my identity"Changes the press's colours, type and layout. Only when you want to; the defaults are fine.
04

Dials

Each dial in config/params.json becomes a control. You don't need to edit the file by hand, but it helps to read it.

One dial
{ "key": "rows", "label": "Rows", "type": "range",
  "min": 4, "max": 160, "step": 1, "value": 48,
  "reason": "One row for each year of the record." }
TypeControl
rangeA slider with a number box: min, max, step.
numberA number box.
toggleOn or off.
selectA choice from options.
colorA colour picker.
textA line of text.

The top of the file also sets the identity's name, the work's title and the paper (A2 is 420 × 594 mm, with a margin). The reason is the identity's reason, and it shows under every control, so write it as carefully as the manifesto.

At 300 PPI for the archival printer, or 150 for proofs.

Share

Copy link

A link to exactly this seed and these dials. Paste it in your log or critique notes.

Downloads are named by seed and key dials, like 001_seed-41822_rows-48_scale-1.6.svg. Resin needs a 3D file the press doesn't make by default; if that's your pathway, ask Codex to add an STL export and explain it.

06

Online

Your press is published on GitHub Pages at https://username.github.io/project/, and updates each time you say "push". It's what the exhibition QR code opens.

  • Visitors can play with it. They can change the dials and download their own seeds. Decide with Codex which dials to show and which to fix before the opening.
  • The About tab is the exhibition text online. It reads manifesto.md and process.md straight from your repository.
  • Check it on a phone before Day 17, when the QR codes are printed.
