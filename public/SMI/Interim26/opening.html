03

Sixty seconds

You'll say this many times tonight. Write it, time it, and then say it without the paper.

Sixty-second introduction
1 · Who: "This is [identity], a [practice] who [position, in one line]."  (10 s)
2 · Why: "[The condition], and why it matters now."                        (15 s)
3 · How: "The work is made by a system that [the key rule, plainly]."       (15 s)
4 · What: "What you're looking at is [the object], and [what to notice]."  (15 s)
5 · Invitation: "The code and the whole process are behind the QR code."   (5 s)

Speak as the author, not in costume. Visitors want to know who is speaking and why it matters; let the identity's own voice stay in the manifesto.
