How briefs work

Each week has one brief, one question and a set of daily deliverables. The worksheets are how you do the work; the brief is what the work has to answer.

  • Due by 3:30. Daily deliverables go in your repository by the end of pin-up, with the day's log entry.
  • Three submissions. Weeks 1 and 2 end in A1 and A2, assessed at their critiques. Weeks 3 and 4 end in A3, the final. See Assessment.
  • Late is still useful. If a day slips, hand it in the next morning and say so in the log. Don't skip it.
