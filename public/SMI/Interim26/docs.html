03

Photographing the work

Day 18, before installation. These photographs are how the work will be seen after the show closes.

Light

Even and soft

  • Daylight from a window, or two lights at about 45° to the work.
  • No direct sun, no flash straight on.
  • Gloss prints and resin: move the lights until the reflections leave the surface.
Camera

Square and steady

  • Phone or camera on a tripod or a stack of books.
  • Lens parallel to the work, centred, so edges stay straight.
  • A grey card or white paper in one frame, to check colour.
Shot list
01The whole work, straight onNothing else in the frame; crop to the edge of the paper or object.
02–04Three detailsClose enough to see the line, the layer or the texture.
05With a scaleA hand or a ruler beside it, so size reads.
06In the zoneAfter installation, with the card, manifesto and QR code.
07ProcessThe machine running, a test beside the final.
08Resin onlyThree angles, against a plain background.

Name files by shot: docs/photos/01_whole.jpg, 02_detail.jpg… Keep them under about 5 MB each in the repository; store originals on the studio drive.
