02

Thursday

Day 4 · Thu 26 Nov. Watch, then write in the identity's voice. The screening is started and supervised by To be confirmed.

  1. 9:30–11:50 · Screening.

    Fight Club (David Fincher, 1999). Adult certificate, with strong violence; you may sit out. If you do, spend the time on references.md: real archives, sites, materials and data.

  2. 11:50–12:30 · Five questions, in your group.

    Two minutes of silent writing on each, then talk. The captain writes down the group's best answer to each.

  3. 12:30–1:15 · Lunch.
  4. 1:15–3:00 · Manifesto workshop, in your group.

    Follow the manifesto worksheet: raw material, a long draft, then the cuts. Each person reads theirs aloud; the group says which line to cut next. Down to 150 words.

  5. 3:00–3:25 · Push.

    Tell Codex "push": manifesto.md version 1, and references.md as far as it's got.

  6. 3:25 · Daily form.

    Then field research for your references continues into the evening: 15 are due with A1 tomorrow.

Five questions · group notes
Group [1/2/3/4] · Day 4 · captain: [name]

1. Which scene would you show someone to explain the film?
2. When did you stop trusting the narrator?
3. The club exists because its rules are spoken aloud. What did the rules make possible?
4. Tyler answers to a feeling. What does each of your identities answer to, with evidence you can show?
5. Which of your rules would be dangerous if no one could question it?
03

Friday

Day 5 · Fri 27 Nov. Test the identity on each other, then submit it. A1 is marked on what you've pushed by 3:30 today; Monday's critique is where you defend it.

  1. 9:30–10:45 · Peer critique.

    Thirteen minutes each, the same shape as a real critique: three to present, eight for questions, two for everyone to fill in a feedback sheet. Use the three-minute format.

  2. 10:45–12:30 · Revise.

    Work through your feedback sheets. Manifesto version 2. References to 15, each one opened and checked.

  3. 12:30–1:15 · Lunch.
  4. 1:15–3:00 · Card and proposal.

    Draft your identity card, and write the one-page proposal below.

  5. 3:00–3:30 · Check and push.

    Go through the A1 checklist. Tell Codex "push", then ask "is it online?". Print the week's log on one A4 sheet for Monday.

  6. Over the weekend.

    Read Foundations. Monday's talk on it is short because you'll have read it.

04

If you're stuck

  1. Ask your group.

    That's what the group is for. Most questions on these two days are about your identity, and your group knows it.

  2. Ask Codex.

    For anything about the repository or pushing, say what you want in plain words. The prompt library has the Day 4 prompts.

  3. Post on WhatsApp.

    Ujjwal may not reply until the evening, or until Monday. Don't wait on an answer: make a decision, write down why in your log, and keep going.

For anything urgent about the room, your health or your safety, go to To be confirmed.
