Deficit Survey, Tank No. 14

Example

Anjana Rao is a surveyor of tank beds, measuring the irrigation tanks Bengaluru has built over since the 1970s.

This A2 pen plot is a survey of one lost tank. Each row of lines is a year of rainfall from 1990 to 2024; the heavier the line, the greater that year's deficit. Nothing is drawn outside the tank's recorded boundary.

The plotter drew every line from a system that reads the rainfall record and obeys three rules from Rao's manifesto. Its heaviest passes put the most ink where the most water is missing.

The work asks what a map of the city would look like if it recorded loss as carefully as ownership.
